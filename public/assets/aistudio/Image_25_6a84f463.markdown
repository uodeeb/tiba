## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the regal heritage, mystical warmth, and sensorial opulence of Upper Egyptian perfumery. Designed for discerning fragrance connoisseurs and high-volume merchant traders across Asyut and nationwide Egypt, the experience merges tactile Oriental maximalism with contemporary high-performance glassmorphism.

The aesthetic evokes nocturnal incense smoke, raw oud cambodi, melted gold leaf, and rare Arabian resins. Visual weight is deliberate, dignified, and tactile: heavy smoked panels hover over deep obsidian depths, framed by luminous gold filaments and glowing calligraphic highlights.

Key aesthetic pillars:
- **Nocturnal Obsidian Substratum:** Deep, warm volcanic blacks evoking charred agarwood embers.
- **Smoked Glass & Liquid Gold:** Multi-tiered frosted obsidian plates, hairline gold metallic seams, and gleaming metallic gradients.
- **Regal Tactile Presence:** Substantial button physics, delicate ambient rim lighting, and ceremonial typography.
- **RTL-Native Royal Flow:** Constructed strictly for right-to-left Arabian optical hierarchy, honoring historic calligraphic weight distribution.

## Colors
The palette is built around charred nocturnal resins, shimmering precious alloys, and ceremonial silk accents. The system operates strictly in dark mode to preserve the rich luminescence of liquid gold and translucent smoked glass.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / Imperial Liquid Gold):** Used for focal CTAs, primary active indicators, royal seals, and illuminated states. Expressed in high-value contexts as a directional linear sheen (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F3E5AB 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #8C6D2D 100%)`).
- **Secondary (`#8C6D2D` / Burnished Bronze Oud):** Used for pressed actions, card shadows, low-key metallic trim, and secondary interactive borders.
- **Tertiary (`#25D366` / WhatsApp Emerald):** Dedicated exclusively to immediate merchant actions, concierge consultations, and order confirmations via WhatsApp.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#090807` / Volcanic Obsidian):** Base background tone infused with faint warm amber undertones to avoid synthetic digital deadness.
- **Elevated Smoked Glass:** Surface tiers `#141210` (Surface Tier 1) and `#171513` (Surface Tier 2), rendered at 80-90% opacity with substantial backdrop blur.
- **Typographic Tones:** Ivory (`#FAF8F5`) for dominant headlines and crisp metrics; Silk Sand (`#CFC6B8`) for body, provenance notes, and metadata; Muted Incense (`#877E71`) for captions and subtle timestamps.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Burnished Gold hairlines (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22)`) and micro-sheen active borders (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.65)`).

## Typography
Typography harmonizes historical Arabesque cadence with high-density commerce utility. 

- **Display & Section Titles:** Rendered using classical Arabic serif cadence (`Noto Serif` / Amiri equivalent) to convey imperial heritage, oud blending mastery, and nobility. Used generously for brand storytelling, collection titles, and sensory notes.
- **Functional Interface & Reading:** Driven by modern geometric proportions (`Plus Jakarta Sans` / Cairo equivalent). This guarantees optimal legibility across Arabic diacritics, transactional statuses, and dense inventory grids.
- **Financial & Match Metrics:** Driven by `JetBrains Mono` for currency amounts (`ج.م`), batch numbers, vintage distillation years, and olfactory match percentages. Tabular numerical alignment maintains zero layout jitter during live merchant price shifts.
- **RTL Typesetting Rules:** Line heights are expanded by 15–20% compared to Western conventions to accommodate vertical Arabic ascenders and descenders without clipping.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a disciplined 12-column fluid grid system on desktop and tablet, collapsing to a single-column tactile rail on mobile devices.

### Layout Rhythm & Fluid Grid
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem`. Maximum content container bounded at 1440px to retain intimate luxury proportion.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Merchant tables convert to horizontally scrolling frosted decks.
- **Mobile (< 767px):** 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`. Touch targets expand to a minimum height of 48px to accommodate ergonomic one-handed Arabian thumb zones.
- **RTL Fluidity:** Spatial margins, drawer docks, badge positions, and iconography originate strictly from the right anchor. Leading margins represent the physical visual start (right), while trailing margins define the resolution (left).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is constructed via smoked, semi-translucent material strata rather than opaque gray surfaces or standard drop shadows. Visual depth mimics multi-layered blackened glass vessels lit from behind by ambient amber embers.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** Pure Volcanic Obsidian (`#090807`). Static, non-elevated ground plane.
- **Tier 1 (Cards, Modules, Feeds):** Smoked Glass (`#141210` at 85% opacity) with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Framed by a 1px burnished gold hairline border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.18)`).
- **Tier 2 (Popovers, Sticky Headers, Merchant Drawers):** Dense Smoked Resin (`#171513` at 92% opacity) with `backdrop-filter: blur(32px)` and gold hairline border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.30)`).
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Sensory Showcases):** Suspended Charcoal Obsidian with `box-shadow: 0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85), 0 0 32px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.

### Ambient Illumination & Borders
- Shadows are tinted with deep umber and bronze (`rgba(12, 8, 4, 0.7)`).
- Hover and focus states never produce harsh drop shadows; instead, they activate an internal radial gold filament aura (`box-shadow: 0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`).

## Shapes
The shape philosophy is understated and architectural. A soft curvature (`roundedness: 1`) preserves the crisp, cut-gemstone elegance of luxury perfume bottles, crystal stopper facets, and gold bar ingots.

- Standard buttons, input fields, and compact tags feature a disciplined 0.25rem (4px) corner radius.
- Product presentation cards, perfume dossier modals, and merchant drawers utilize `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px).
- Pills are strictly reserved for batch status pills, WhatsApp instant triggers, and olfactory concentration indicators (Extrait de Parfum / دهن عود خالص).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Imperial CTA:** Rich liquid gold gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F3E5AB 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #8C6D2D 100%)`) with obsidian text (`#090807`), semibold weight, 1px gold micro-sheen border. Hover scales luminance with an internal amber glow.
- **Secondary Ghost Gold:** Smoked glass backing (`#141210`), 1px hairline border (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`), Ivory text (`#FAF8F5`). On hover, border opacity shifts to 100% with gold ambient spread.
- **WhatsApp Royal Concierge:** Saturated WhatsApp Emerald (`#25D366`), white typography, soft emerald edge illumination (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px rgba(37, 211, 102, 0.2)`).

### Cards & Fragrance Showcases
- Smoked obsidian glass with 1px gold hairline borders.
- Top-right corner (RTL start) reserved for royal seal badges or vintage harvest tags.
- Imagery styled with dramatic under-lighting and subtle warm dark vignetting.
- Lower card strata houses monetary values in monospace JetBrains Mono, accompanied by `ج.م` notation.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: Smoked plate (`#141210`) at 70% opacity.
- Border: 1px subtle bronze (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.2)`), transitioning smoothly to active radiant gold (`#D4AF37`) on focus.
- Placeholder text in Silk Sand (`#CFC6B8`), right-aligned text flow with custom golden trailing search/selection glyphs.

### Chips & Olfactory Tags
- Faceted micro-tags for top/heart/base notes (e.g., دهن عود هندي، عنبر ملكي، زعفران).
- Subtle smoked background, 1px perimeter border, `space-xs` padding, and mono-scaled text.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Diamond-faceted geometry (rotated squares) honoring geometric Islamic pattern roots.
- Inactive: 1px burnished bronze outline with smoked core. Active: Solid `#D4AF37` fill with obsidian pip.

### Merchant Platform Modules
- Real-time wholesale bulk pricing matrices, tier-based commission cards, and provenance certificates built with micro-sheen golden tabular lines and distinct column alignments.
